# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and relevant to the project. Disagreement about code, design, historical interpretation, or implementation is fine; harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and targeted abuse are not.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary. Serious concerns can be reported privately through the repository's security/contact channels when appropriate.
